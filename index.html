Where you live in Italy decides how much culture you can reach.

Local loans per public library, 2022. A public library in Emilia-Romagna made 10,137 on average, one in Campania 149: 68 times fewer.

One book in these piles is 100 loans a year. Source: ISTAT, Indagine sulle biblioteche pubbliche e private 2022, table 4.12. Every region, shelf by shelf

Italy has more than thirteen thousand libraries and four and a half thousand museums, but they are not spread, used or priced evenly. This story follows public data from ISTAT and the Ministry of Culture region by region, and adjusts for population wherever the data allows.

Follow a region

Pick it here or on the map. It stays marked in every chart below, and its value rides on every folder tab.

Corrado Consiglio, Salvatore Di Marzo and Alice Picco. Information Visualization, University of Bologna, 2023/24.

Supply

Counting libraries rewards big provinces. Per inhabitant, the map changes.

A map of raw counts mostly shows where people live: Rome has the most libraries simply because it has the most residents. ISTAT's 2012 table gives both the count and the rate per 10,000 inhabitants for every province, so the two can be set side by side on the same boundaries. The rate is the fairer measure of how close a library is to each resident.

Reach

One municipality in four has no library of its own.

We placed each of the 13,319 geolocated libraries in the 2022 register inside the municipal boundary that contains it. 2,040 of the 7,899 municipalities in the boundary file contain none. This gap does not follow the north–south line: Abruzzo and Piemonte have the highest shares, Sardegna and Sicilia some of the lowest.

Use

Southern libraries lend a fraction of what northern ones do.

Having a library is not the same as using one. ISTAT's 2022 survey reports the average number of local loans per library that answered, which already removes the effect of how many libraries a region has. We use public libraries for the ranking: in Lazio a few private libraries account for 5.5 million loans, which would push its all-library average to 18,218.

Reading

Where libraries lend less, fewer people read.

The reading survey is already a share of the population (people aged 6 and over, reading outside school and work), so regions can be compared directly. The two panels share one order: regions ranked by the share who read at least one book.

Time

The gap narrowed because the north read less.

ISTAT asks the same question every year: did you read at least one book in the last twelve months, for reasons other than school or work? Setting 2011 beside 2021 shows whether the regional gap is closing, and how.

Price

Price is not where the gap is.

Of the 4,559 museums in the Ministry's open register, 2,556 state their entry conditions: 1,370 free and 1,186 with a ticket. Free entry does not follow the north–south line.

Events

Open data shows the Ministry's reach, not all of culture.

The Ministry of Culture publishes the events held in its places as open data: 30,541 events in 1,112 cities. Counted per resident, they say less about how much culture a region has than about how much of it runs through the Ministry.

Findings

What the data says

  1. Use is the widest gap.

    Libraries are only somewhat scarcer in the south (1.8 against 2.3 per 10,000), but they lend 17 times less. The gap in use is far wider than the gap in supply, so more buildings alone would not close it. See loans per library

  2. Small places are left out, north and south.

    One municipality in four has no library; the share is highest in Abruzzo (56%), Piemonte (42%) and Liguria (41%). Rural libraries lend 9 times less than urban ones. See reach

  3. Reading follows the same line.

    The four largest southern regions have the fewest readers (27–29%), and the regions where libraries lend least read least. See reading and loans

  4. Counts mislead; rates help.

    Absolute counts crown Rome and Milan. Per inhabitant, Trieste, the Alpine provinces and parts of Sardinia lead, and the densest commuter provinces fall behind. See the two maps

  5. Distance is rarely the barrier.

    3.5 million people live in a municipality without a library, but for half of those municipalities the nearest one is under 4 km away. Real distances are confined to Alpine valleys and small islands. See the distance map

  6. Fewer people read than ten years ago, everywhere but Umbria.

    The north–south gap in reading narrowed from 16.5 to 13.9 points, but because northern regions lost more readers, not because southern ones gained. See the gap over time

  7. Open data has blind spots.

    National datasets miss what autonomous regions run themselves: Sicily and Trentino-Alto Adige almost vanish from the Ministry's events data. A low number can mean "not recorded here", not "not there". See events

Method & sources

How the numbers were made

Data
ISTAT tables on libraries (2010–2022), library loans (2019–2022) and reading habits (2011–2021); the ICCU register of libraries; the Ministry of Culture's open "Luoghi della cultura" dataset on dati.cultura.gov.it (museums, archives, libraries, entry prices). All files are in the repository.
Per capita
Libraries per 10,000 inhabitants are ISTAT's own rates for 2012. The repository has no population figures for other years, so we did not compute rates ourselves. Loans are ISTAT's average per library; reading figures are already shares of the population.
Headline figures
68× = 10,137 ÷ 149 (loans per public library, Emilia-Romagna ÷ Campania, 2022). 17× = 7,631 ÷ 441 (north-east ÷ south). 9× = 12,655 ÷ 1,429 (cities ÷ rural areas). One in four = 2,040 ÷ 7,899 municipalities.
Areas
North: Piemonte, Valle d'Aosta, Lombardia, Trentino-Alto Adige, Veneto, Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Liguria, Emilia-Romagna. Centre: Toscana, Umbria, Marche, Lazio. South & islands: Abruzzo, Molise, Campania, Puglia, Basilicata, Calabria, Sicilia, Sardegna (ISTAT's "Mezzogiorno").
Distance
For each municipality with no library inside its boundary, the straight-line distance from the centre of its largest polygon to the nearest geolocated library in the 2022 register. Travel distance is longer, especially in the mountains and to islands.
Population
ISTAT resident population by municipality on 1 January 2023 (POSAS), used to count residents without a library and to express events per resident. A compact copy is in data/population/comuni_2023.csv.
Reading over time
ISTAT's yearly tables on reading, 2011–2021. The layouts differ from year to year; the build script reads the share of people aged 6+ who read at least one book from each, and checks that 2021 matches the table used in the Reading chapter.
Events
Events published per city in the Ministry of Culture's open events data, matched to municipalities by name. The dataset covers places linked to the Ministry, so regions with their own heritage administration (Sicily, Trentino-Alto Adige) are under-represented.
Reading the charts
Every chart has a "How to read it" line and a data table. The page was redesigned with a plan for testing how readers understand it: usability test plan.
Processing
Data gathering and cleaning in Python notebooks (in data/). The figures on this page are rebuilt from those files by scripts/build_story_data.py, which also simplifies the province boundaries and runs the point-in-polygon count.
Licence
Data from dati.cultura and ISTAT: CC BY 4.0. Project: CC0 1.0, by Alice Picco, Salvatore Di Marzo and Corrado Consiglio.
Built with
D3.js, GeoJSON, HTML and CSS. No tracking.
